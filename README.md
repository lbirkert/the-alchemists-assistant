## Core Idea

You play the role of a failed stock market trader. Previously you were
employee of the month but due to the AI-bubble crash now unemployed.

Desperately you begin seeking a new job and find a listing for a "laboratory assistant".

Upon arrival you notice that your employer is actually a weird alchemist 
and that your task is to brew potions.

It's your goal to make his customers and most importantly him happy.
This will yield fat bonuses which you need along your bad salary.

With this money you can upgrade your alchemy equipment to brew new
potion types and make money faster.

Your goal is it to become employee of the month again.
